About the service

The Bestlink SMS2 system is the internal student management and research system of Bestlink College of the Philippines. It runs at bestlinksms2portal.com and its subdomains, including crad.bestlinksms2portal.com and student.bestlinksms2portal.com.

Who may use it

The system is for students, faculty, staff and panelists of the college who have an account issued by the school. Keep your login details private. You are responsible for activity under your account.

Acceptable use

  • Use the system only for school academic and administrative purposes.
  • Do not try to access records, accounts or backups that are not yours or not assigned to you.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, scan, or break into the system or its services.
  • Report any suspected security problem to the system administrator.

Google Drive backup feature

The administrator may connect a Google account to store the school's system backups in Google Drive. The connection uses only the drive.file permission, so SMS2 can access only the backup files it creates. Only authorized administrators may connect or disconnect this feature. The administrator can disconnect at any time, and can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

See the Privacy Policy for how this data is handled.
