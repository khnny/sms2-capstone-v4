Privacy Policy

Bestlink SMS2 Portal

Last updated: November 28, 2026

What this service is

The Bestlink SMS2 system is the internal student management and research system of Bestlink College of the Philippines. It is available at bestlinksms2portal.com and its subdomains, including crad.bestlinksms2portal.com and student.bestlinksms2portal.com.

Google account access

The SMS2 backup feature can connect to a Google account that belongs to the school administrator. It asks for one permission only:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission lets SMS2 create and update only the backup files that SMS2 itself creates in that Google Drive. It does not let SMS2 read, change or delete any other file in the Drive.

What we use it for

We use this access for one purpose: to store the school's own system backups (database exports and related system files) in the administrator's Google Drive, so the school can restore the system if needed.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data, or any data stored in Google Drive, with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or for any purpose other than the backup feature above.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to other systems or third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data to train machine learning or AI models.

Storage and access

Backups are stored only in the Google Drive of the authorized administrator. Access to the SMS2 system and its backups is limited to authorized school staff. Google access tokens are kept by the server and are not shown to users.

Disconnecting and deleting

The administrator can disconnect the Google account from SMS2 at any time. The administrator can also revoke SMS2's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files can be deleted from the administrator's Google Drive at any time.
